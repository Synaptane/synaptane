# Getting help

Synaptane is an **alpha / preview** project, provided as is and maintained on a best-effort
basis by a single person. There is **no guaranteed support and no commercial support**.
Answers come when time allows, often from other users.

Where to go, in this order:

1. **Documentation**: the [`docs/`](docs/index.md) folder covers installation, administration,
   day-to-day use and the API. Many questions are answered there.
2. **Questions**: ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/synaptane/synaptane/discussions)
   (Q&A category). Search first: someone may have asked already. Ideas and open-ended
   proposals are welcome there too.
3. **Bugs**: if you can reproduce a problem, open an
   [issue](https://github.com/synaptane/synaptane/issues/new/choose) with the bug report form.
   Include `synaptane --version` and the output of `synaptane doctor`, and remove secrets
   from logs.
4. **Security vulnerabilities**: never in public. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
5. **Code of Conduct reports**: never in public either. Use the same private form as for
   vulnerabilities, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). As a fallback, use GitHub's
   abuse report: <https://github.com/contact/report-abuse>.

English or French are both fine.
