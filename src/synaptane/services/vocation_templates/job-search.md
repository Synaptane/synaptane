# Space purpose

## What this space is for, and for whom

Tracking my job search: the companies I am targeting, the applications, the interviews and what I learn from them. Read by me and my AI assistants, to prepare each step.

## What goes here

- One sheet per company: business, culture, news, contacts, why it interests me.
- One page per application: position, job ad (link and copied text), date sent, version of the CV and cover letter, status, follow-ups.
- Interview notes: interviewers, questions asked, answers to improve, next steps.
- My pitch: quantified achievements, answers to the usual questions.
- My network: people met, what we talked about, when to follow up.

## What does not go here

- Administrative documents (unemployment office, contracts): only the due dates and where to find them.
- Job ads of no interest: leave in the inbox with the reason.

## Folder organization

- `companies/`: one sheet per company.
- `applications/`: `YYYY-MM-DD-company-position.md`.
- `interviews/`: `YYYY-MM-DD-company.md`, linked to the application.
- `pitch/`: achievements, questions and answers, CV.
- `network/`: one page per person or per event.

## Conventions

- Language: English; keep the text of a job ad in its original language.
- File names: lowercase with hyphens, dates in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- Application: a "Status" field at the top (to send, sent, interview, rejected, offer) and the date of the next follow-up.

## What matters most

- Dates: sending, follow-ups, interviews. A forgotten follow-up is a missed opportunity.
- Link each application to its company sheet and its interviews.
- Keep the lessons of each interview for the next one.
