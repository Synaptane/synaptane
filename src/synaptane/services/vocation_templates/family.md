# Space purpose

## What this space is for, and for whom

The family's practical memory: paperwork, health, home, holidays, schools. So that everyone quickly finds a piece of information, even one someone else wrote down. Read by the adults of the family and their AI assistants.

## What goes here

- Paperwork: where each document is, its expiry date, how to renew it (identity card, passport, driver's license, vehicle registration, insurance).
- Health: doctors and their contact details, vaccinations, current treatments, appointments.
- Home: contracts (energy, internet, insurance), tradespeople, maintenance, warranties, user manuals.
- Holidays: ideas, bookings, packing lists, memories.
- School and activities: timetables, contacts, enrolments.
- Household finances: due dates, organizations, contacts (not the codes).

## What does not go here

- No password, card PIN or banking credentials.
- Full numbers of identity or social security documents: only note where to find them.
- Personal messages without useful information: leave in the inbox with the reason.

## Folder organization

- `paperwork/`: one page per person and one per vehicle.
- `health/`: one page per person, and `health/contacts.md`.
- `home/`: contracts, tradespeople, maintenance, appliances.
- `holidays/`: one page per trip, `YYYY-destination.md`.
- `children/`: school, activities, one page per child.
- `budget/`: due dates, organizations.

## Conventions

- Language: English, plain; first names rather than "Mr" or "Mrs".
- File names: lowercase with hyphens, no accents.
- Always date things: appointments, due dates, expiry dates (YYYY-MM-DD format).
- A "To do" section at the top of the page while a procedure is under way.

## What matters most

- Confidentiality: nothing that would allow someone to steal an identity or access an account.
- Due dates: an expiry or cancellation date must never get lost.
- Accurate contact details (phone numbers, addresses).
- Verified pages: contract and insurance pages are verified every year, before they expire.
