# Space purpose

## What this space is for, and for whom

Everything there is to know about a project carried out for a client: the context, the commitments, the exchanges and the progress. Read by the project team and by anyone who has to step in for it at short notice.

## What goes here

- The client sheet: organization, contacts and their role, way of working.
- The scope, the contract, the schedule and the milestones.
- Minutes for each meeting: attendees, decisions, actions (who, what, by when).
- Change requests and how they were settled.
- Deliverables and their approval status.
- Risks and open points.

## What does not go here

- Detailed amounts and financial terms if the space is open to people who must not see them: leave in the inbox with the reason.
- Personal exchanges or exchanges unrelated to the project.

## Folder organization

- `client/`: client sheet, contacts.
- `scope/`: scope, contract, schedule.
- `meetings/`: `YYYY-MM-DD-subject.md`.
- `deliverables/`: one page per deliverable.
- `follow-up/`: risks, open points, change requests.

## Conventions

- Language: the client's; the client's proper names and acronyms as they are.
- Tone: factual, without judgment on people.
- File names: lowercase with hyphens, dates in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- Minutes: attendees, decisions, actions with owner and due date.

## What matters most

- The commitments made and their dates: never lose track of them.
- The confidentiality of the client's information.
- An up-to-date "To do" page per deliverable or per meeting.
- Verified pages: the scope and the contact list have an owner and are verified every 3 months.

## Vocabulary

- UAT: user acceptance testing, the client's approval of the deliverables.
- Steering committee: the project's decision-making body.
