# Space vocation

## What this space is for, and for whom

My personal second brain: what I learn, what I read, my ideas and my projects, so I can find them again and connect them. Read by me and my AI assistants.

## What goes here

- Reading notes: book, article, podcast, with the source and what I take away from it.
- Sheets on topics that interest me, completed over time.
- My personal projects: goal, next steps, ideas.
- Loose ideas, attached to the closest topic.
- Recipes, tips and everyday procedures.

## What does not go here

- Administrative paperwork and health information (a "family" space suits them better).
- Passwords and codes: never.

## Folder organisation

- `topics/`: one sheet per topic, enriched with each new note.
- `reading/`: one page per important book or article.
- `projects/`: one page per ongoing project; `projects/archive/` for finished ones.
- `ideas/`: ideas not yet attached to a topic.
- `practical/`: recipes, tips, procedures.

## Conventions

- Language: English; quotations in their original language, in quotation marks, with the source.
- Tone: personal, in the first person.
- File names: lowercase with hyphens.
- Topic sheet: a summary at the top, then the dated notes, and links to neighbouring topics.

## What matters most

- Connect the notes to each other: an isolated idea gets lost.
- Keep the source of each quotation.
- Prefer enriching an existing sheet rather than creating a new one.
