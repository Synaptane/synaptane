# Space vocation

## What this space is for, and for whom

The marketing team's memory: our brand, our messages, our campaigns and what they taught us. Read by the team, the sales people and the AI assistants that help with writing.

## What goes here

- The brand platform: positioning, promise, tone, editorial guidelines.
- Personas and customer segments.
- One page per campaign: goal, target, channels, budget, schedule, results, lessons.
- Key messages per offer, arguments and answers to objections.
- Competitive intelligence, with the date and source of each piece of information.
- Press, agency and partner contacts (without unnecessary personal data).

## What does not go here

- Contact files or e-mail lists: they stay in the e-mailing tool.
- Drafts that led nowhere: leave in the inbox with the reason.
- Figures without a source.

## Folder organisation

- `brand/`: brand platform, tone, guidelines, visual assets.
- `audiences/`: personas, segments.
- `offers/`: one page per offer or product, with its key messages.
- `campaigns/`: one page per campaign, `YYYY-MM-campaign-name.md`.
- `intelligence/`: one page per competitor.
- `content/`: editorial calendar, ideas, published articles.

## Conventions

- Language: English; keep taglines written in another language as they are.
- Tone: the one in the guidelines (see `brand/`), clear and concrete.
- File names: lowercase with hyphens.
- Campaign page: goal, target, messages, channels, schedule, budget, results, lessons.

## What matters most

- Quantified, dated results, with their source: they are what guides the next steps.
- Messages consistent with the brand platform.
- Fresh intelligence: date each piece of information.
- Verified pages: the brand platform and the key messages have an owner and are verified every year.

## Vocabulary

- CPL: cost per lead. CPA: cost per acquisition.
- MQL, SQL: marketing-qualified lead, sales-qualified lead.
